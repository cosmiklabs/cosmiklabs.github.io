A different approach to creating 3D structures

A Cosmik project

Create structures for worlds real or imagined.

Bring an idea and develop it with Tectra’s AI. Together, work out the forms, materials and details using a construction system of connected parts and rules that Tectra can check as the design evolves.

01 / The idea

Describe the space.
Shape what comes next.

You bring the vision. Develop the details together.

Start with what you want to create, even if you haven’t worked out every measurement. The agent helps develop the missing details and handles the construction and materials.

No vertex dragging or manual material assignment. You guide the design through conversation.

From everyday spaces to imagined worlds, Tectra focuses on built and assembled structures.

02 / The foundation

Built from
relationships.

A structure is more than its final shape.

Tectra works with connected parts and the relationships between them. You or your agent can define rules for how the parts should fit together, and Tectra checks those rules as the design develops.

You don’t need a complete specification before you begin. The details can develop with the idea.

03 / The conversation continues

An AI that can build,
inspect and refine.

01

Explore the result

Move through the structure in the viewport. The agent can create named views that help you find your way around.

02

Point to what matters

Select a part or drill down to a surface and reference it in chat. Add a capture of your current view to show what you’re seeing.

03

Shape the next revision

Ask for changes and keep developing the same design. The agent can inspect the structure and materials from its own viewpoints, too.

Selecting a part starts a conversation. It isn’t manual geometry editing.
